How to play

File Frenzy is a choose-your-own-adventure story. Read each page, then pick what happens next.

  • Choices are buttons. Click or tap them, or press Tab to move between them and Enter to choose. You can also press the number keys 1–9 to pick a choice.
  • Questions: if you get one wrong, you’ll get a hint and can try again. No one is keeping score!
  • “But why?!” links lead to short lessons. Read them, then press Back to carry on.
  • Notebook: every lesson you find is saved in your notebook, so you can read it again any time.
  • Back takes you to the previous page. Press Backspace or B as a shortcut.
  • Settings let you change colours, font, text size and spacing, and turn on read aloud.

One puzzle uses the PRONOM website (opens in a new tab). If you can’t get online, there’s a button that shows you the answers.

Your progress is saved automatically on this device.
